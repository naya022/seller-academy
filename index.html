Behind the design

The business problem

New sellers on a global ads sales team need to run a confident first client call fast. Long product decks slow ramp and don't build the habits that win deals: discovery before pitching, tying products to the client's goal, and closing on a clear next step. This sprint replaces a day of slides with 20 minutes of practice.

Learning objectives

  1. Match a client's business goal to the right ad product (Search, Display, Video or Autopilot) in 4 of 5 cases.
  2. Ask open discovery questions that uncover goal, audience, current marketing, budget and success measures before recommending a product.
  3. Deliver a goal-based commercial pitch and secure a dated next step in a live practice call.
  4. Choose the best response to common client objections in scenario-based decisions, scoring 80% or higher.

Production AI role-play prompt

This prototype runs a scripted intent engine so it works anywhere with no login. In production, the same rubric would drive a large language model with this system prompt.

You are Maya Ortiz, owner of Mesa Verde Coffee Roasters, a small-batch roaster with one café and an online store. You are talking to a new ads seller on their first call.
Facts to reveal only when the seller asks: goal = 60 online orders a month (now about 20); best customers = home brewers aged 28 to 45 who find you at the farmers market; current marketing = occasional social posts, no ads; budget = about 1,500 a month in local currency; success = more orders in your store dashboard.
Behavior: be friendly but busy. If the seller pitches a product before asking at least three discovery questions, push back. Favor recommendations tied to online orders. Agree to a next step only if it has a specific date.
After the call, step out of character as "Nova" and score the seller 0-100 on Discovery (40), Listening (15), Product fit (25) and Next step (20). Give two strengths and one thing to try next time, in plain language.

Design choices

  • Built with Action Mapping: every screen practices a behavior from a real first call.
  • One selling method throughout (Learn, Match, Pitch, Commit) so product knowledge, pitch and close are practiced as one workflow.
  • Global audience: market selection localizes currency; plain language and no idioms that don't translate.
  • Accessible: full keyboard support, visible focus, screen-reader live regions, reduced-motion support.
  • Brightline Ads is a fictional company created for this portfolio sample.
